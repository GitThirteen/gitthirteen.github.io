    Hi there, name's Michael! Happy to have you here.

    I am a master's student at TU Wien in Vienna, currently majoring in Visual Computing. My primary focus lies in Computer Graphics and Visualization, but I dabble often enough in other areas for it to not really matter.

    Down below you will find all my current projects, publications, and whatever other stuff I feel like sharing with the world. I am also currently working on an interactive, introductory online textbook & slidedeck about physically-based rendering called Lightcraft. You can find the current version for completely free, along with my visualization projects, on the right side of the header!

    Some milestones:

    • I obtained my B.Sc. in Media Inf. & Visual Computing in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Michael Wimmer.
    • From 2022 - 2026, I aided the development of the DigiFit4All & Dev0land CS education projects at eduLAB.
    • I ran my first half marathon this year and survived! (wohoo!)
